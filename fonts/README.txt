GenZ Mode fonts
---------------
Two files are picked up automatically when you drop them in this folder.

HEADINGS and SUBHEADINGS  (Unique Typeface, free variable condensed)
  genz-font.woff2   or   genz-font.ttf   or   genz-font.otf

BODY TEXT  (Nohemi, free variable)
  nohemi.woff2      or   nohemi.ttf      or   nohemi.otf

Until your files exist, the page falls back to free lookalikes from Google Fonts:
  headings -> "Anybody" (condensed variable)     body text -> "Outfit" (geometric sans)
