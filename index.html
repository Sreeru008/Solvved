01 · Discover

Swipe through people who actually fit.

Set your preferences once: age, career, location, values and deal-breakers. Soulvved suggests people who match, in a card stack that feels like the apps you already love.

  • ✓Suggestions built around your preferences
  • ✓Like, pass or shortlist in a single swipe
  • ✓A compatibility score on every profile
02 · Together

Your parents are in the loop. Not in your way.

Parents browse detailed profiles and shortlist for you. You see what they liked, they see what you liked, and every pick is labelled with who chose it.

  • ✓See the profiles your parents liked and shortlisted
  • ✓Parents see your likes and shortlist too
  • ✓Their picks live in their own tab, never pushed on you
03 · Match

It's a match. Families, meet.

When two people match, parents are told straight away and can see exactly who their child matched with. They can then talk to each other in a private parent-to-parent chat.

  • ✓Parents see every match, and with whom
  • ✓Parent-to-parent chat opens after a mutual match
  • ✓Phone numbers stay hidden until both families agree
